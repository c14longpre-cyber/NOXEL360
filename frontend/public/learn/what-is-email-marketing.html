What Is Email Marketing? A Simple Explanation

In one sentence: Email marketing is sending targeted messages directly to a list of subscribers who've opted in, remaining one of the highest-return marketing channels available.

Despite the rise of social media, email marketing has consistently remained one of the highest-ROI marketing channels — largely because, unlike social followers, an email list is owned directly by the business, not filtered through a platform's algorithm.

Common Types of Marketing Emails

  • Newsletters — regular updates, content, or news sent to a subscriber list
  • Promotional emails — announcing sales, offers, or new products
  • Automated sequences — a series of emails triggered by an action (like signing up), sent automatically over time
  • Transactional emails — order confirmations, receipts, account notifications
  • Re-engagement emails — reaching out to inactive subscribers

Why Owning the List Matters

Social media followers are subject to a platform's algorithm deciding whether they even see your posts. An email list bypasses that entirely — a message sent to your list arrives directly, without a third-party algorithm deciding its reach. This is a significant, often underappreciated advantage.

Key Metrics to Track

Open rate — the percentage of recipients who open an email. Click-through rate — the percentage who click a link inside it. Unsubscribe rate — how many opt out after a given email, a useful signal for content relevance and frequency.

Consent and Legal Requirements

Email marketing is regulated — laws like CAN-SPAM (US) and GDPR (EU) require clear consent to email someone and an easy, functioning way to unsubscribe. These rules vary by region, so understanding what applies to your audience matters before building a serious email program.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is email marketing still relevant with social media available?

Unlike social media followers, an email list is owned directly by the business — not subject to a platform's algorithm deciding who sees your message.

Is it legal to email anyone who gives you their email address?

No — regulations like CAN-SPAM and GDPR require clear consent and an easy way to unsubscribe. Rules vary by region, so checking applicable regulations matters.

What's a good email open rate?

This varies significantly by industry, but averages commonly fall somewhere in the 15-25% range — context and list quality matter more than chasing a single universal benchmark.
