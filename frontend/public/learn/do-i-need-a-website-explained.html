Do I Need a Website for My Business?

🕐 5 min read 📊 Beginner 📁 Business Questions 📅 Published July 24, 2026 🔄 Updated July 24, 2026

In one sentence: For nearly every business, yes — a website is the one online asset you fully control, unaffected by a social platform's algorithm, policy changes, or an account suspension.

This comes up constantly, especially from businesses doing well on social media or marketplaces alone. The honest answer depends on your specific situation, but there are a few things worth knowing either way.

The Core Reason: You Don't Own Social Media

A Facebook page, Instagram account, or marketplace listing is built on rented land — the platform sets the rules, changes the algorithm, and can suspend an account without much recourse. A website is the one place you fully control.

💡 Did You Know?

Businesses relying solely on social media have occasionally lost their entire audience overnight due to account suspensions or policy changes — a risk a self-owned website doesn't carry.

When a Website Might Genuinely Not Be Essential Yet

A very early-stage business testing an idea, or one operating entirely through an established marketplace with its own strong built-in traffic, might reasonably delay building a full website — though this is usually a temporary stage, not a permanent strategy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I run a business successfully with just social media?

Some do, especially early on, but it means depending entirely on a platform you don't control — a real, ongoing risk worth weighing.

Is a website expensive to set up today?

Not necessarily — modern website builders and platforms have made basic websites accessible at low cost compared to a decade ago.

Does having a website actually bring in more customers?

It can, particularly through search visibility, though a website alone doesn't guarantee traffic — it needs to be found, which is where SEO comes in.

Can I have both a website and social media?

Yes — this is the most common and generally recommended approach, using each channel for what it does best.

Do I need a website if I only sell through a marketplace like Etsy or Amazon?

It's less urgent, though marketplaces don't give you full control over the customer relationship or branding the way an owned website does.

How quickly can a basic business website be set up?

With modern website builders, a simple, functional site can often be live within days, not months.

Is a one-page website enough for a small business?

For many small businesses, yes — a single, well-organized page covering key information can be entirely sufficient to start.

Key Takeaways

  • A website is the one online asset a business fully controls.
  • Social media and marketplaces carry real platform-dependence risk.
  • Modern tools have made basic websites accessible and affordable.
  • A website and social media typically work best together, not as alternatives.
  • Very early-stage businesses might reasonably delay, but not indefinitely.
