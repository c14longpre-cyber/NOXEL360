How Much Does a Website Cost?

🕐 5 min read 📊 Beginner 📁 Business Questions 📅 Published July 24, 2026 🔄 Updated July 24, 2026
On This Page
  • Rough Cost Tiers
  • Don't Forget Ongoing Costs

  • FAQ
In one sentence: A basic small business website can cost as little as $10-30/month using a modern website builder, while a custom-developed site can run from a few thousand to tens of thousands of dollars depending on complexity.

This is one of the most common questions, and the honest answer is: it depends heavily on what you actually need, not just what's technically possible.

Rough Cost Tiers

  • DIY website builder — $10-40/month (Squarespace, Wix, Shopify)
  • Template + freelancer setup — $500-3,000 one-time, plus hosting
  • Custom-developed site — $5,000-50,000+ depending on complexity
  • Enterprise-scale platform — significantly more, for large-scale needs
📚 Official Sources
  • Shopify — Website Cost Guide · a practical cost breakdown reference
💡 Did You Know?

A large share of small business websites today are built on platforms with monthly subscription pricing rather than a large upfront payment, spreading cost over time instead of a single big expense.

Don't Forget Ongoing Costs

Beyond the initial setup, ongoing costs typically include hosting, a domain name renewal, and periodic content or maintenance updates — a website is rarely a true one-time expense.

What Actually Drives Cost Up

Custom design, e-commerce functionality, integrations with other business systems, and ongoing content creation are the factors that most commonly push cost significantly higher than a basic informational site.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I build a website myself without paying a developer?

Yes — modern website builders are designed specifically for non-technical users to build a functional site without coding.

Is a cheap website worth it for a small business?

Often yes, at least to start — a simple, functional site is generally better than none, and can be upgraded later as the business grows.

Do I need to pay for hosting separately?

It depends on the platform — many website builders include hosting in their subscription; custom-developed sites usually need separate hosting.

Why do custom websites cost so much more than DIY builders?

Custom development involves bespoke design, functionality, and integrations tailored specifically to a business, requiring significantly more skilled labor.

Are there ongoing costs after building a website?

Yes — hosting, domain renewal, and periodic updates are typically ongoing, even for a simple site.

Is it worth paying for a professional designer?

For businesses where visual brand impression matters significantly, yes — though many small businesses start with templates and upgrade later.

Key Takeaways

  • Basic small business websites can cost as little as $10-40/month with modern builders.
  • Custom-developed sites range from a few thousand to tens of thousands of dollars.
  • Ongoing hosting and domain costs continue beyond the initial build.
  • Custom design and complex functionality are the main cost drivers.
  • A cheap, functional site is generally better than no site at all.
