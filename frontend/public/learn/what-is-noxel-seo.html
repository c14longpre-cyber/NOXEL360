What Is NOXEL SEO? A Simple Explanation

In one sentence: NOXEL SEO is an AI-powered SEO audit and intelligence platform that scans a website for over 50 technical, content, and AI-visibility signals, then uses an AI Copilot to explain issues and suggest fixes in plain language.

Search itself is changing — people now find answers through Google, but also ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini. NOXEL SEO was built specifically to audit visibility across all of that, not just traditional search rankings.

How It Works, in Three Steps

  1. Enter a URL — any public page, yours or a competitor's
  2. Get a full scan — 50+ signals checked in under 60 seconds
  3. Ask the AI Copilot — get a prioritized, plain-language action plan

Beyond Traditional SEO

NOXEL SEO explicitly measures visibility across AEO (Answer Engine Optimization), GEO (Generative Engine Optimization), SXO, and E-E-A-T signals — not just the traditional Google-ranking-factor checklist most SEO tools focus on.

💡 Did You Know?

NOXEL SEO's free audit completes in under 60 seconds, checking over 50 technical signals — meta tags, Core Web Vitals, schema, security headers, and more — in one single scan.

🧩 See It in Action with NOXEL360

You can run a free audit on your own site directly at noxelseo.com — no account required to see your first results.

Key Features

  • Deep technical audit (meta tags, canonical, hreflang, redirects, and more)
  • Core Web Vitals analysis with prioritized fixes
  • AI Copilot powered by Claude, explaining issues in plain language
  • Schema and structured data validation
  • Reports and AI recommendations in 115 languages, with RTL support
  • Scan history to track SEO progress over time

Frequently Asked Questions

Is NOXEL SEO free to use?

Yes — it's free to start, with no credit card required for the initial audit, and paid tiers unlocking more advanced features.

What does NOXEL SEO check that other tools don't?

It explicitly measures visibility in AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini, alongside traditional Google SEO — most competing tools only cover the latter.

What is the AI Copilot in NOXEL SEO?

An AI assistant, powered by Claude, that can explain any scan finding in plain language and generate a prioritized, actionable fix plan.

How long does a NOXEL SEO audit take?

Under 60 seconds for a complete scan covering 50+ technical, content, and visibility signals.

Does NOXEL SEO support languages other than English?

Yes — reports and AI recommendations are available in 115 languages, including full right-to-left (RTL) language support.

Is NOXEL SEO part of a larger platform?

Yes — it's one of the live products within the broader NOXEL360 ecosystem.
