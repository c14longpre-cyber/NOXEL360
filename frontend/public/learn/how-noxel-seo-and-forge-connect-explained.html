How NOXEL SEO and Forge Connect

In one sentence: NOXEL SEO and NOXEL Forge are separate standalone products, each with their own domain, that share a common NOXEL360 account and design system — covering different, complementary parts of a site's SEO health.

They solve different problems: NOXEL SEO audits and fixes on-site technical and content issues; NOXEL Forge helps build off-site credibility through backlinks. Together, they cover both major halves of SEO.

Two Complementary Halves of SEO

NOXEL SEO handles the on-site half — technical health, content structure, Core Web Vitals, and AI-engine visibility. NOXEL Forge handles the off-site half — earning credible backlinks that build a site's external trust signals.

💡 Did You Know?

SEO is often described as having two major halves — on-page factors you control directly, and off-page factors like backlinks that depend on other sites. NOXEL SEO and Forge were built specifically to address one half each.

One Shared Account and Design System

Despite being separate products on separate domains, both connect back to a shared NOXEL360 account, letting a user move between them without managing entirely separate logins or billing relationships.

🧩 See It in Action with NOXEL360

Both products are accessible through the same NOXEL360 account — explore them at noxelseo.com and noxelforge.com.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to sign up for NOXEL SEO and Forge separately?

They're accessed through a shared NOXEL360 account system, streamlining the experience across both products.

Which one should I use first, NOXEL SEO or Forge?

Most people start with NOXEL SEO to identify and fix on-site issues, then consider Forge to build off-site credibility through backlinks.

Is using both products necessary for good SEO?

Not strictly necessary, but since they address complementary halves of SEO, using both provides more complete coverage than either alone.

Are NOXEL SEO and Forge priced together or separately?

They're separate products with their own offerings, though connected through the same NOXEL360 account system.
