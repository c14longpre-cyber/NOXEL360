Shopify vs WooCommerce: What's the Difference?

In one sentence: Shopify is a fully hosted, all-in-one e-commerce platform; WooCommerce is a free plugin that turns a self-hosted WordPress site into an online store, trading convenience for more control.

Shopify: Hosted and Managed

Shopify handles hosting, security, and technical maintenance entirely — you sign up, choose a design, add products, and launch, without ever touching a server. This convenience comes with a monthly subscription cost and somewhat less flexibility for deep customization compared to a self-hosted approach.

WooCommerce: Free, Self-Hosted, Flexible

WooCommerce is a free plugin for WordPress, giving you an e-commerce store built on a platform you fully control. This offers more customization freedom, but requires managing your own hosting, security updates, and technical maintenance — either yourself or through a developer.

Side-by-Side Comparison

ShopifyWooCommerce
HostingIncluded, managedSelf-hosted, your responsibility
Cost structureMonthly subscriptionFree plugin + hosting + extensions
Technical maintenanceHandled by ShopifyYour responsibility (or a developer's)
CustomizationGood, within Shopify's ecosystemVery high, full WordPress flexibility
Best forFast setup, less technical involvementFull control, existing WordPress sites

How to Think About Which One Fits

If launching quickly with minimal technical overhead matters most, Shopify's managed approach removes a lot of friction. If deep customization, full data ownership, or an existing WordPress site are priorities, WooCommerce's flexibility — at the cost of more hands-on management — is often the better fit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is cheaper, Shopify or WooCommerce?

WooCommerce itself is free, but hosting, a domain, and any premium plugins add up — total cost can end up comparable to Shopify depending on the specific setup and scale.

Which is easier for a beginner?

Shopify is generally considered more beginner-friendly, since hosting and technical maintenance are handled automatically as part of the platform.

Can I switch from WooCommerce to Shopify later, or vice versa?

Yes, migration is possible in both directions, though it typically requires careful planning around product data, orders, and customer records to avoid data loss.
