What Is Local SEO? A Simple Explanation

In one sentence: Local SEO is the practice of optimizing a business's online presence so it appears prominently in local, nearby searches — like "coffee shop near me" or a Google Maps listing.

General SEO helps a website rank in search overall. Local SEO specifically targets searches with local intent — a real difference in strategy for any business with a physical location or a defined service area.

The Foundation: Google Business Profile

A complete, verified Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) is the single most important local SEO asset — it's what powers a business's appearance in Google Maps and the local "3-pack" results shown above organic search results for local queries.

Key Local SEO Factors

  • NAP consistency — Name, Address, and Phone number matching exactly across every online listing
  • Customer reviews — quantity, quality, recency, and how the business responds to them
  • Local citations — mentions of the business across relevant local directories
  • Location-specific content — pages genuinely relevant to the specific area(s) served
  • Proximity — how close the business is to the person searching (a factor Google weighs directly)

Why Local SEO Behaves Differently From General SEO

Local search results are influenced heavily by the searcher's actual location — the same query can produce completely different results depending on where someone is standing. This is why a strong Google Business Profile and consistent local information matter more here than in general organic SEO.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a Google Business Profile free?

Yes, creating and managing a Google Business Profile is free, and it's one of the highest-impact steps for local search visibility.

Does local SEO matter for online-only businesses?

Generally less so — local SEO is most relevant for businesses with a physical location or a defined local service area.

How important are customer reviews for local SEO?

Very — review quantity, quality, and recency are significant factors in local search ranking, alongside how a business responds to them.
