Why no prices here?
Each module's pricing lives on its own site (noxelseo.com, noxelforge.com), so you always see the current plans, not a copy that can go stale. NOXEL SEO and NOXEL FORGE each have their own sign-in and their own plans; your NOXEL360 account covers the dashboard, NOXEL NEXUS and the Learn library.

Common questions

Do I need separate accounts for each module?
Yes, for now. NOXEL SEO and NOXEL FORGE each have their own sign-in and their own plans on their own sites. Your NOXEL360 account covers the dashboard, NOXEL NEXUS and the Learn library.
Can I use just one module?
Yes. Each module stands on its own: using NOXEL FORGE doesn't require NOXEL SEO, and vice versa.
Is NOXEL NEXUS a paid add-on?
No. It's included with your NOXEL360 account at no extra cost.
Is NOXEL SPECTRA free?
Yes, today. Its image tools are online at app.noxelspectra.com and its video tools are in beta. Paid plans will be announced on noxelspectra.com before they start.
