FAQ

Common questions

Do I need separate accounts for each module?
No. NOXEL SEO and NOXEL Forge both connect to your one shared NOXEL360 account, and future modules will too.
Can I use just one module?
Yes. Each module stands on its own — enabling NOXEL Forge doesn't require NOXEL SEO, and vice versa.
Why don't you list exact prices here?
Each module's pricing lives on its own site (noxelseo.com, noxelforge.com), so you always see the current plan — not a copy that can go stale.
Is NOXEL Nexus a paid add-on?
No. It's included with your NOXEL360 account at no extra cost.
When is NOXEL Spectra launching?
Spectra is in development. Its features and pricing will be published on noxelspectra.com when it launches, and it will work with your existing NOXEL360 account.
