FAQ

Common questions

Do I need separate accounts for each module?

No. NOXEL SEO and NOXEL Forge both connect to your one shared NOXEL360 account.

Can I use just one module?

Yes. Each module stands on its own: enabling NOXEL Forge doesn't require NOXEL SEO, and vice versa.

Why don't you list exact prices here?

Each module's pricing lives on its own site (noxelseo.com, noxelforge.com), so you always see the current, accurate plan, not a copy that can go stale.

Is NOXEL Nexus a paid add-on?

No. It's included with your NOXEL360 account at no extra cost.
